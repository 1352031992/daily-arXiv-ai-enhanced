*Jose Marie Antonio Miñoza,Erika Fille T. Legara,Christopher P. Monterola*

Main category: cs.LG

TL;DR: 本文精确地将神经网络训练过程映射为Hamilton-Jacobi初值问题的搜索：每个梯度步对应选择粘性Hamilton-Jacobi方程的最优初值，通过Hopf-Cole传播子拟合观测数据。该对应关系对log-sum-exp层精确成立，ReLU、sigmoid、SiLU和GELU均为其极限、梯度或矩形式；在深度和宽度上的复合也是精确的，有限深度误差在联合极限下消失。对残差网络、Transformer和循环网络（RNN/LSTM/SSM）该对应是结构性的，具有明确的近似误差。单一变形参数ε统一了四种视角（网络、热带代数、粘性PDE、凸优化）。定量结论包括：最优泛化率O(n^{-1/(d+2)})、对抗鲁棒性由ε控制、反向传播对应残差网络的协态方程（Pontryagin最大值原理）、以及O(N)复杂度的影响函数（softmax归因权重π_j），其熵景观随ε增大经历折叠分岔。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 作者试图建立神经网络与现代PDE理论之间的精确数学对应关系，目标是用Hamilton-Jacobi方程的成熟数学工具来统一理解网络训练、推理和泛化，从而获得超越经验观察的定量保证。

Method: 通过将梯度下降每一步视为选择粘性Hamilton-Jacobi方程的Hopf-Cole传播子初值，建立网络层与PDE算子之间的一一对应；利用ε作为统一变形参数，将网络、热带代数、粘性PDE和凸优化四个视角纳入交换图框架，并推导各网络结构的近似误差界。

Result: 获得了多个定量结果：最优泛化率O(n^{-1/(d+2)})、ε控制的对抗鲁棒性、反向传播作为Hamilton系统的协态方程、与数据内蕴维度一致的缩放指数、以及O(N)影响函数（softmax归因权重），其熵景观在ε增大时发生折叠分岔。

Conclusion: 该框架将神经网络训练系统地嵌入Hamilton-Jacobi PDE理论，为深层网络提供了统一的数学基础，并揭示了归因权重的熵景观分岔行为这一新的动态现象。

Abstract: In this paper, training a neural network is identified, exactly, as a search through Hamilton--Jacobi initial-value problems: each gradient step selects the initial data of a viscous Hamilton--Jacobi equation whose Hopf--Cole propagator best fits the observations; at inference, the input is the spatial point at which that solution is evaluated and the initial condition is already encoded in the weights. The correspondence is exact for log-sum-exp layers, with ReLU, sigmoid, SiLU, and GELU each an exact limit, gradient, or moment of the same object, and exact in composition across depth and width, with a quantified error at finite depth that vanishes in the joint limit. It is structural for residual networks, transformers, and recurrent networks (RNNs, LSTMs, SSMs), each discretizing the same class of equations, at a named and quantified approximation error. A single deformation parameter $\varepsilon$ unifies all four perspectives (network, tropical algebra, viscous PDE, convex optimization) in a commutative diagram closed under Lipschitz conditions. Quantitative consequences include: the minimax optimal generalization rate $O(n^{-1/(d+2)})$ for fixed $t$; adversarial robustness controlled by $\varepsilon$; backpropagation as the co-state equation of the Hamiltonian system for residual networks (Pontryagin Maximum Principle); scaling exponents consistent with data intrinsic dimension via PDE quadrature; and a closed-form $O(N)$ influence function (softmax attribution weights $π_j$) whose entropy landscape undergoes fold bifurcations as $\varepsilon$ increases, each merging attribution basins.

### [21] [Density theorems for $\text{GL}_n$ via Rankin-Selberg $L$-functions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.13682)
*Jared Duker Lichtman,Alexandru Pascadi*

Main category: math.NT

TL;DR: 本文为GL_n上尖点自守表示在违反广义Ramanujan猜想时的密度定理提供了新证明，弃用Kuznetsov型迹公式，改用L-函数技术，改进了Blomer在逐点界阈值附近的结果。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究GL_n上尖点自守表示在某一位置处违反广义Ramanujan猜想时的密度分布规律，旨在通过新的技术路径改进已有结果。

Method: 不再依赖Kuznetsov型迹公式，而是采用L-函数分析技术来建立密度定理。

Result: 获得了新的密度定理，在逐点界的阈值附近改进了Blomer近期取得的成果。

Conclusion: 基于L-函数的技术路径在该问题上优于传统迹公式方法，能获得更精细的密度结果。

Abstract: We obtain density theorems for cuspidal automorphic representations of $\text{GL}_n$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ which fail the generalized Ramanujan conjecture at some place. We depart from previous approaches based on Kuznetsov-type trace formulae, and instead rely on $L$-function techniques. This improves recent results of Blomer near the threshold of the pointwise bounds.
